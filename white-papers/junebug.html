    What is Junebug?

    Junebug is a personal AI operating system — small, local, specialized agents instead of one giant chatbot doing everything.

    Why Junebug?

    Most AI assistants run one massive general-purpose model for every task: expensive at scale, overpowered for most of what you actually ask it, and your data lives on someone else's servers. Junebug takes the opposite approach — light, local models, each one scoped to do a specific job well, running entirely on your own machine.

    • Local-first. Your data never leaves your hardware.
    • Specialized over generalized. A model built to edit your website doesn't need to also summarize physics papers — narrow scope means better results for less compute.
    • You're always in the loop. Nothing external happens — no emails sent, no code pushed — without your explicit approval.
    • Modular by design. Add a new agent for a new task without touching what already works.

    Built on OpenClaw.

    Where Junebug is headed

    Junebug starts as one agent doing one job well. The architecture is built to grow from there.

    • A roster of specialists. Beyond the first agent, future agents each handle a narrow domain — one for code, one for correspondence, one for whatever comes next — coordinated by a lightweight router instead of one model trying to be everything.
    • Fine-tuned, not just prompted. As real usage surfaces what each agent is bad at, that becomes training data — small LoRA fine-tunes on top of small base models, so specialists get sharper over time instead of just bigger.
    • Still entirely yours. More agents, more capability, same rule: it runs on your hardware, and nothing leaves the machine without you saying yes.
    • A pattern, not just a project. The goal isn't just "my assistant" — it's a reusable harness anyone can point at their own machine, their own tools, their own data.

    Junebug isn't trying to out-scale the big labs. It's betting that a handful of small, well-trained specialists — under your control, on your own machine — beats one expensive generalist you don't fully trust.
